Deterministic rules work locally. Categories describe the evidence available; they are never confidence percentages.

OBSERVED STATE

Pre-selected optional choice

An optional control was checked when first seen, with no observed interaction. An earlier choice or saved preference may explain it.

MEASURED UI

Unequal choice prominence

Compare nearby acceptance and rejection controls. A measured difference is a signal to inspect, not a conclusion about intent.

OBSERVED BEHAVIOR

Suspicious countdown reset

Record an increase after descending samples. A legitimate offer extension can also produce this behavior.

OBSERVED CHANGE

Late-visible fee

A matching fee appears after the initial visible baseline. Verify the final total and possible recalculations.

HEURISTIC

Possible confirm-shaming

A choice label matches a local English phrase rule. Context, sarcasm and unrecognized wording remain limitations.
